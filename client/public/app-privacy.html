Privacy Policy — Divya Urja Kranti App

Effective date: 19 October 2026 (updated: employee duty tracking) · Divyanshi Digital Services Pvt Ltd, Bank of India Building, Naubatpur, Mashurhi Road, Patna, Bihar 801109

The Divya Urja Kranti app ("the app") is used by Divyanshi Solar's registered partners, shop partners, field staff and office staff to register solar customers, carry out site surveys and installations, register partner shops and view commission. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Who can use the app

The app is for business use by adults (18+) who hold a Divyanshi Solar account. It is not intended for children.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, district/state and password (stored only as a one-way hash on our server).
  • Customer details entered by partners: customer name, mobile number, address, PIN code, electricity consumer number, DISCOM, monthly bill, roof type and proposed plant capacity — collected so the customer's PM Surya Ghar / rooftop solar application can be processed.
  • Photos: site, installation, meter and shop photos taken with the in-app camera. The app does not read your photo gallery.
  • Precise location: collected only while you use the app, when you take a work photo, register a shop or tap "Use current location". It is used to geo-tag work and to show registered shops on a map. The app does not collect location in the background.
  • Employee duty location (Divyanshi staff only): when an employee taps Duty In, the app takes a selfie and their location, and then records their location about once a minute — including when the app is closed or not in use — until they tap Duty Out. A notification ("On duty") is shown the whole time. This is used for attendance, to show the office where field staff are, to plan jobs, and to calculate kilometres travelled. No location is collected when the employee is off duty. Partners and shop owners are never tracked.
  • Device information: device model and Android version, recorded with your login session for security.

How we use it

  • To process solar applications, schedule and verify installations, and comply with DISCOM and PM Surya Ghar scheme requirements.
  • To calculate and pay partner commission.
  • To verify that field work and shop registrations were done on site.
  • To keep accounts secure.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Sharing

Customer and installation details are shared only as needed to deliver the service: with the electricity distribution company (SBPDCL/NBPDCL), the national PM Surya Ghar portal, banks handling a customer's solar loan, and our installation teams. Map tiles in the app are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which receives the map area being viewed but no personal details. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on our servers in India. Work saved while offline is kept in the app's private storage on your phone until it uploads, then removed. Login tokens are stored in the phone's secure storage.

Retention

Employee GPS location points are kept for 1 year and then deleted automatically; duty in/out times and daily kilometre totals are kept as attendance records. Customer, installation, commission and invoice records are kept for as long as required under Indian tax, GST and scheme rules (generally at least 8 years). Login sessions expire after 90 days of issue.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. You can also request deletion without the app — see Account deletion. Deleting your account closes your login immediately and removes your location data. Records we must keep by law (customer applications, commission payments, invoices) are retained as described above.

Your choices

You can deny camera or location permission in Android settings; the features that need them will then not work. You may ask us to correct your details at any time.
